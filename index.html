Founded by entrepreneur Bruce Michaël Lagacé Gallant, BMLG Group grew from a conviction: real estate should be built with rigour, long-term vision and a sense of responsibility. Today, BMLG Group Inc. is a holding company connecting complementary businesses across the property lifecycle—from owning assets to operating, maintaining and improving them.
