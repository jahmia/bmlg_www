# BMLG Group Website Project

## Project Overview
This the website for  BMLG Group, a Canadian real estate company that owns, manages, and maintains properties through three subsidiaries: BMLG Property Inc., BMLG Services Inc., and BMLG Soldier Memorial Inc.

## Goals
The site should present bmlg, build credibility. The main call to action is “Book a free consultation”.

## Target Audience
The website serves three main groups:
- **Tenants:** people looking for rental housing.
- **Partners:** investors and co-developers interested in collaborating on real estate projects.
- **Clients:** property owners or managers who need services such as property management, renovation, or maintenance.

## Content
- Crate content from company details in about.txt (fr)
- Include Home, About, Services, Contact. For the homepage, include a hero section with a headline and CTA, a short services overview, and a contact form. - Create separate pages for privacy policy & terms of services.
- Make it easy for each audience to find their path, with separate entry points for tenants, partners, and clients.
- Use a professional, trustworthy tone that reflects rigor, long-term vision, and responsibility.

## Brand and style:

## Technical requirements
- Set up three connected files: index.html, style.css, and app.js.
- Fully responsive for mobile and desktop, fast loading, accessible (proper contrast and alt text), with SEO basics (meta titles, descriptions, headings). - Language: English and French.
- Keep in mind that places like "New Brunswick" doesn't have to be translated
