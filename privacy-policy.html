How we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard personal information.

Effective date: November 16, 2026

BMLG Group ("BMLG," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit our websites, use our online portals, interact with our property management services, or communicate with us, including by text message.

By using our websites, portals, or services, or by providing personal information to us, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

You may browse certain parts of our websites without providing personal information. We collect information directly from you, automatically through use of our websites and portals, and from third parties where permitted.

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

You may provide personal information when requesting information, scheduling a showing, submitting a rental application or maintenance request, creating an account, subscribing to communications, completing surveys, or contacting us by email, phone, or text.

This information may include your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, date of birth, employment information, rental history, and details relevant to your request or tenancy.

1.2 Mobile Phone Numbers and Text Messaging

When you provide a mobile number through our website, portals, applications, or forms, we may use it to send and receive text messages related to property management services. These may include account alerts, maintenance updates, lease reminders, community news, urgent notifications, and other service communications.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties.

1.3 Information Collected Automatically

We may automatically collect an IP address, browser and device information, pages visited, time spent, navigation paths, and referring websites or search engines. We may also use cookies and similar technologies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to respond to inquiries and applications, manage tenancies and maintenance, send service communications, create and manage accounts, send permitted marketing communications, improve our services, analyze usage, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

We may create aggregated or anonymized reports that do not identify individuals.

3. Text Messaging (SMS) Communications

When you provide your mobile number and consent to text messages, we may send account and tenancy updates, lease renewals, payment reminders, community news, urgent notifications, events, and responses to inquiries.

Message and data rates may apply and message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance. You may also contact us directly to request removal from text communications.

We honor opt-out requests promptly. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our websites use cookies and similar technologies to enhance functionality, recognize returning visitors, understand usage, and personalize content. You may change your browser settings to refuse cookies or receive notices. Disabling cookies may affect some features.

We may use third-party analytics tools to understand how visitors interact with our websites.

5. Third-Party Services

Third-party providers may support property management portals, payments, email and SMS delivery, analytics, identity verification, or credit checks. These providers may process information on our behalf for the services we offer.

We take reasonable steps to require appropriate confidentiality and data-protection safeguards. We do not sell or rent personal information or share mobile numbers or SMS consent data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

6. Disclosure of Information

We may disclose personal information to service providers under confidentiality obligations, to comply with law or legal process, to protect rights or safety, in connection with a merger or asset transfer subject to appropriate protections, or with your consent or direction.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information, including secure infrastructure, firewalls, HTTPS/SSL, controlled access, and confidentiality obligations. No internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to meet the purposes described here, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. When no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to securely delete or anonymize it.

9. Your Privacy Rights

BMLG complies with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and other applicable Canadian privacy laws. Under Canadian law, you generally may access personal information we hold, request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information, withdraw consent subject to legal or contractual restrictions, and ask how information is collected, used, or disclosed.

To exercise these rights, contact us using Section 12. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe. You may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

10. Marketing Communications

For marketing emails, use the unsubscribe link in the message. For text messages, reply STOP or contact us directly.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
