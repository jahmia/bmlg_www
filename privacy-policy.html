Privacy,
with care.

A clear overview of how information is handled when you visit the BMLG Group website or contact us.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

BMLG Group Inc. (“BMLG”, “we” or “us”), based in New Brunswick, respects the privacy of people who visit this website or contact us. This notice explains what information may be handled through this site.

Information you choose to send

The contact form prepares a message in your device’s email application. It does not submit form entries to or store them on this website. If you send the email, BMLG receives the information in it and may use it to reply to your enquiry.

Language preference and technical logs

If available in your browser, this site uses local storage to remember your English or French language preference. The website host may also keep basic request logs as part of operating the site.

Use, retention and questions

Information sent to BMLG is used to respond to the enquiry and is kept only as reasonably needed for that purpose or to meet legal requirements. For questions or requests about personal information, contact us at contact@bmlggroup.com.

Updates

We may revise this notice when the site or its practices change. The date above indicates the latest revision.
