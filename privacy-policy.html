Privacy policy.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy describes how BMLG Group Inc. (“BMLG”, “we” or “us”), based in New Brunswick, handles personal information when you visit this website or contact us. It applies to this website and enquiries made through it.

Information you provide

The contact form prepares a message in your device’s email application; it does not transmit or store form entries on this website. If you choose to send the email, your email provider processes it and BMLG may use the information to respond to your enquiry and continue that conversation.

Technical information and third-party resources

The website host may process basic technical information in server logs to operate and protect the site. This website loads photographs from Unsplash; when your browser requests those images, the provider may receive technical data such as your IP address. Its own privacy terms apply. This website does not intentionally use advertising or analytics trackers.

Use, sharing and retention

We use information you choose to send to respond to you, manage your enquiry and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers where needed to support communications or hosting, or when required by law. We keep enquiries only as long as reasonably needed for these purposes and apply reasonable safeguards.

Your choices and questions

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information. For questions or requests about privacy, contact BMLG Group at contact@bmlggroup.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the website, our practices or legal requirements. The date at the top indicates when it was last updated.

Before publishing: Confirm that this policy reflects BMLG Group’s actual data practices and obtain legal review for applicable jurisdictions.
