Privacy policy.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy describes how BMLG Group (“BMLG”, “we”, “us” or “our”), based in New Brunswick, handles personal information when you visit this website or contact us. This page is general information and should be adapted to the company’s actual practices before publication.

Information you provide

The contact form on this website prepares an email in your device’s email application. It does not send or store your form entries on this website. If you choose to send the email, your email provider will process it and BMLG Group may use the information in it to respond to your enquiry and continue that conversation.

Technical information and third-party resources

Our hosting provider may process basic technical information in server logs to operate and protect the website. This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and photographs hosted by Unsplash; those providers may receive technical data, such as your IP address, when your browser requests those assets. Their own privacy terms apply to their services. This site does not intentionally use advertising or analytics trackers.

Use, sharing and retention

We use information you choose to send to respond to you, manage the enquiry, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers where needed to support communications or website hosting, or when required by law. We keep enquiries only as long as reasonably needed for these purposes and apply reasonable safeguards.

Your choices and questions

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. For questions or requests about privacy, contact BMLG Group at contact@bmlggroup.com. We will handle requests in accordance with applicable requirements.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the website, our practices, or legal requirements. The date at the top indicates when it was last updated.

Before publishing: Confirm that this policy accurately reflects BMLG Group’s actual data practices and obtain legal review for applicable jurisdictions.
